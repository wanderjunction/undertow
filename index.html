Undertow

静かな波のような、アンビエントでミニマルな四つ打ちテクノを、止めるまで作り続けるプレイヤーです。

決まった曲を流すのではなく、再生するたびにその場で新しい曲が生まれます。キー、テンポ、展開は毎回ちがい、同じ曲は二度と流れません。録音された音は使わず、キックからシンギングボウルの響きまで、すべてブラウザの中でリアルタイムに合成しています。

曲は波とパッドだけの静かな始まりから、水面下の鼓動のようなキックがだんだん浮かび上がり、四つ打ちの流れへと満ちていきます。8 小節ごとに変化が起きても、一度に変わるのは一つまで。気づくか気づかないかくらいの変化が続くので、長く聴いていても飽きにくく、邪魔にもなりにくい作りです。セクションとは別に、曲全体も数分かけて近づいたり遠ざかったりします。

使い方

  • 再生 / 停止：真ん中のボタン、または Space キー
  • チェンジ（右）：いまの曲がフェードで引いていく間に、新しい曲が始まる
  • auto（左）：曲ごとに曲調を選び直す。同じ曲調は続かない
  • 曲調：下のボタンで固定。鳴っている曲調のボタンは、その色でうっすら光る
  • 音量：右の数字は、いまの曲の経過時間
  • 1 曲を 15〜25 分ほど聴くと、静かな区間で自然に次の曲へつながる

Undertow

A player that keeps making ambient, minimal four-on-the-floor techno, like quiet waves, until you stop it.

Nothing is prerecorded. Every time you press play, a new track is composed on the spot — the key, the tempo and the arrangement change each time, and the same track never plays twice. There are no samples: everything from the kick to the singing bowls is synthesized live in your browser.

Each track begins quietly with waves and pads. A kick rises slowly, like a heartbeat under the surface, until the four-on-the-floor flow fills in. Something changes every eight bars, but never more than one thing at a time. The changes are so subtle you may barely notice them, so it stays fresh over long listening without getting in the way. On top of that, the whole track slowly drifts closer and farther away over several minutes.

How to use

  • Play / Stop: the center button, or the Space key
  • Change (right): the current track fades out while a new one begins
  • auto (left): picks a new mood for every track, never the same one twice in a row
  • Moods: the buttons below lock a mood. The one playing glows softly in its own color
  • Volume: the number on the right is the elapsed time of the current track
  • After about 15–25 minutes, one track flows naturally into the next during a quiet passage

Moods

tide
Dub minimal over quiet waves. Chords wrapped in deep echo repeat as the waves come and go. The heart of Undertow
drift
Slow, floating ambient. The beat stays under the surface and soft pads take the lead. Long, dark reverb
echo
Leaning toward dub techno. Short chords and rimshots bounce through deep, slightly tape-worn echoes
dawn
Daybreak. The only major-key mood, with clear chords and xylophone-like sparkles
moon
Moonlit water. Over soft chords, pure tones slowly swell and fade like waves
pulse
The most techno of all. A firmer kick, busy hi-hats and a moving bassline
glass
Clear and cold. High, short sounds and metallic sparkles in a wide space
deep
The deep sea. Sinking into low drones and very long reverb
calm
Pure relaxation. Singing bowls ring slowly over a pulse like a distant heartbeat
glide
Gliding. Bass, percussion and arpeggio cycles drift slightly out of step and flow on without a break
ember
Embers. Warm, dusky chords and gently wistful harmony
fog
Four-on-the-floor in the fog. The kick is never heard, yet it makes everything else breathe on each beat. No low end — it just drifts

Tempos are ranges. Each play picks a tempo within the range.
